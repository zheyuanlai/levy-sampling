\section{Introduction}
The target of interest is a Boltzmann distribution $\pi(x)\propto \exp[-\beta V(x)]$ for a potential energy function $V$ and inverse temperature $\beta$. In metastable landscapes, local diffusion-based samplers can equilibrate rapidly within one basin while exchanging mass between basins on much longer time scales. This separation of time scales is a central obstacle in enhanced sampling because observable estimates can look locally stable while still carrying large basin-population bias.

This study reframes the repository as a Journal of Chemical Physics enhanced-sampling experiment pipeline. The scientific question is whether finite-activity nonlocal jumps, paired with a stationary L\'evy-score correction, can improve equilibrium sampling diagnostics without changing the target distribution. The intended use is thermodynamic sampling and observable estimation, not physical reaction kinetics.
