\section{It is correct for the right reason}
\label{sec:move2}

Two controls rule out the alternative explanations that (i) the correction merely happens to help
relaxation, or (ii) any nonlocal jump of the right length would do.

\paragraph{Invariance: the corrected generator holds the target it is given.} Initializing the
ensemble \emph{at} the Gibbs reference and integrating forward, a target-preserving generator should
not move the basin occupations, whereas an invariant-measure-changing one should drift. LSC-CP stays
at the reference on both the triple well and the 10D transformed M\"uller--Brown landscape, tracking
jump-free local Langevin, while raw CP---identical jumps, no correction---drifts off by an order of
magnitude (Table~\ref{tab:move2-invariance}). This is the empirical face of the continuous-time
stationarity identity: the correction is a change of the \emph{invariant law}, not of the transient.

\paragraph{Geometry, not mere nonlocality.} The transformed M\"uller--Brown potential embeds the 2D
landscape in ten dimensions and mixes coordinates by a fixed orthogonal $\bm B$ ($x=z\bm B^{\!\top}$,
$\varepsilon=0.5$); the jump shells are \emph{lifted} from the latent minima through $\bm B$. The
fair control keeps the same jump-length distribution but randomizes directions
(\texttt{random\_matched\_length\_control}). Geometry-matched lifted jumps reach basin-TV $0.012$
and cover all basins fastest, whereas the random-direction control of the same length reaches only
$0.124$ (Table~\ref{tab:move2-geometry}); the uncorrected lifted jumps sit at $0.181$. Acceleration
therefore comes specifically from inter-basin geometry, and fidelity from the correction---two
distinct effects.

For reference, the full efficiency benchmark on this landscape gives LSC-CP basin-TV $0.012$ against
BAOAB $0.219$, MALA $0.156$, PT $0.100$, HMC $0.067$ and local $0.110$; LSC-CP is both the most
faithful and, at $5.9\times10^3$ ESS/s, competitive on wall-clock here because the two jump vectors
per step keep the L\'evy-score quadrature cheap.
