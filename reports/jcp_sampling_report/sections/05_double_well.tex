\section{Double-well experiment}
The one-dimensional double-well experiment checks whether the L\'evy-score-corrected jump channel improves basin exchange while preserving symmetric Boltzmann weights. It includes local Langevin-style baselines, Metropolis-adjusted variants, HMC, parallel tempering, and shell jumps across the two wells. The main diagnostics are basin KL, population error, observable bias, and compute-normalized ESS.
