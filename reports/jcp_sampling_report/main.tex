\begin{abstract}
Metastability limits the efficiency of local stochastic dynamics for equilibrium Boltzmann
sampling. This internal report evaluates the L\'evy-score-corrected compound-Poisson dynamics
(LSC-CP): local overdamped Langevin, plus finite-activity nonlocal jumps, plus a stationary
L\'evy-score drift $S_{\nu,\varepsilon}$ that cancels the invariant-measure defect of the raw jumps
so the Boltzmann target $\mu_\varepsilon\propto\exp[-V/\varepsilon]$ is preserved. Every experiment
serves one step of a single argument: the correction is a real invariant-measure fix rather than a
speed trick; it is correct for the right reason (a change of invariant law, and geometry that
matters); the jump support governs which modes communicate while correctness stays automatic; the
payoff regime is trapped, high-dimensional, and lattice-structured (ManyWell scaling, a
barrier-height sweep validating the barrier-free inter-well rate, and a real force-field alanine
run); and the additive form has an honest boundary (continuous symmetries and steep steric barriers,
where Metropolis-corrected darting takes over). We do not claim a blanket ``orders of magnitude''
gain: on smooth low-dimensional landscapes well-tuned local samplers and parallel tempering match or
beat LSC-CP, and every ESS is reported with its target-fidelity error. Named quantities are produced
from the CSV/JSON artifacts under \texttt{results/jcp\_sampling}; negative and tie results are
retained.
\end{abstract}
