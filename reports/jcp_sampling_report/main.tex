\begin{abstract}
Metastability limits the efficiency of local stochastic dynamics for equilibrium Boltzmann sampling. This report evaluates finite-activity jump diffusions equipped with a stationary L\'evy-score drift correction (the L\'evy-score-corrected compound-Poisson dynamics, LSC-CP), which is designed to preserve the target Boltzmann distribution while introducing nonlocal inter-basin transport channels. Following the manuscript, the three main examples---a double well, a triple well, and a transformed M\"uller--Brown landscape embedded in ten dimensions---compare local Langevin, raw compound-Poisson (CP), and LSC-CP, so that raw CP isolates the invariant-measure defect that the correction removes. Defensive ablations quantify the finite-step (timestep) bias, the jump scale/intensity mechanism, the role of jump support and direction, and start-at-equilibrium invariance; a compute-normalized efficiency benchmark and two additional landscapes (four-well graph ablation, high-dimensional ManyWell) complete the study. All numerical claims in the generated results, tables, and figures are produced from CSV/JSON artifacts in \texttt{results/jcp\_sampling}; failed or negative outcomes are retained rather than hidden.
\end{abstract}
